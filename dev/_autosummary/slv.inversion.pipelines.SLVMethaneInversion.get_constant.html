slv.inversion.pipelines.SLVMethaneInversion.get_constant#

SLVMethaneInversion.get_constant(obs)[source]#

Background concentration for each obs (config.background).

Obs whose background is missing (outside the ct_stilt product, rolling-baseline gaps) are dropped here rather than filled: fips vectors reject NaN, and aggregate_obs_space reduces the obs to match.

Return type:

Vector
