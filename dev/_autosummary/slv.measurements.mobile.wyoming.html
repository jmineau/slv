slv.measurements.mobile.wyoming#

Wyoming mobile lab: Aeris CH4/C2H6 and vehicle met readers, enhancements and C2/C1 ratios, and a wind-barb map of a drive.

Functions

calculate_enhancements(data[, window])

Add CH4_base/C2H6_base (lair.background.rolling_baseline()) and the enhancements over them, CH4_ex/C2H6_ex.

enhanced_R_and_ratio(data[, window])

Add the rolling window correlation R of the CH4 and C2H6 enhancements and their ratio C2/C1 (after calculate_enhancements()); the analyzer's own columns are kept as R_aeris and C2C1_aeris.

merge_aeris_met(aeris, met)

Merge aeris and met dataframes, interpolating met data to match aeris timestamps

plot_windbarbs(data, ws, wd[, ax, x, y])

Draw wind barbs from the speed column ws and direction column wd at each (x, y); makes a PlateCarree axes when ax is not given.

read_aeris(file)

Read an Aeris analyzer CSV: CH4 (ppb) (converted from ppm), C2H6 (ppb), the analyzer's R and C2/C1, indexed by Time_UTC.

read_met(file)

Read a vehicle met-station file: position (latitude, longitude, Altitude (m)), temperature, humidity, pressure, GPS-corrected wind and vehicle speed, indexed by Time_UTC, plus the source filename.

wyomingMap(data, param[, ax, extent, tiler, ...])

Map a drive coloured by param (a column of the merged GeoDataFrame from merge_aeris_met()), with every windskip-th wind barb and optional map tiles.

slv.measurements.mobile.wyoming.calculate_enhancements(data, window='1h')[source]#

Add CH4_base/C2H6_base (lair.background.rolling_baseline()) and the enhancements over them, CH4_ex/C2H6_ex.

window is the baseline window: anything pd.Timedelta takes, or a number of hours (rolling_baseline’s old integer-hours convention; a bare number would otherwise be read as nanoseconds and make every enhancement zero).

slv.measurements.mobile.wyoming.enhanced_R_and_ratio(data, window='30s')[source]#

Add the rolling window correlation R of the CH4 and C2H6 enhancements and their ratio C2/C1 (after calculate_enhancements()); the analyzer’s own columns are kept as R_aeris and C2C1_aeris. Modifies data in place.
